The habits that move a writing band

Plan for five minutes

Five minutes deciding your position and your two body paragraphs saves ten minutes of rewriting. Candidates who start writing immediately usually produce the weakest structure.

Develop, don't list

Two ideas explained properly beat five ideas mentioned. Each body paragraph wants a claim, a reason, and either an example or a consequence.

Leave two minutes to check

Scan only for your own known errors — articles, plural -s, verb tense. Most candidates can find three or four real mistakes in two minutes.
