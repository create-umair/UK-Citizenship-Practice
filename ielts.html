Which IELTS do you need?

Three different tests share the IELTS name. Sitting the wrong one is an expensive mistake.
If you are applying for UK settlement or citizenship: the English requirement is usually met with an approved Secure English Language Test at CEFR B1 or above in speaking and listening, or through an exemption such as a degree taught in English. IELTS Life Skills B1 is one of the options people use, but the approved list changes. Check your specific route on gov.uk before booking anything. This is separate from the Life in the UK test, which you will normally also need to pass.

How the band score works

Each skill is scored from 0 to 9. The overall band is the mean of the four, rounded to the nearest half band.
BandDescriptionWhat it means in practice

Rounding

  • An average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band — 6.25 becomes 6.5.
  • An average ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band — 6.75 becomes 7.
  • An average ending in .125 or .375 rounds down. Half a mark in one skill can move your overall band.
  • Many institutions set a minimum in each skill as well as an overall minimum. Check both.
