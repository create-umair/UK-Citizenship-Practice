Tips for the real English requirement

Know which test you need

Requirements vary by visa or citizenship route — some accept a degree taught in English instead of a separate test. Check gov.uk for your specific route.

Practise speaking aloud

Many approved tests include a speaking component. Practising conversation out loud, not just reading silently, makes a real difference.

Use everyday situations

Reading tenancy agreements, NHS letters, or job listings in English is excellent real-world B1-level practice alongside this module.
