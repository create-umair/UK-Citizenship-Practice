Reading the passages the way the test wants

Not Given is not "probably false"

If the passage does not state it, the answer is Not Given — even when the claim is obviously true in the real world. Judge only what is on the page.

Watch the absolutes

Words like all, never, proved and only in a question statement often turn a True into a False. Check whether the passage really makes that strong a claim.

Paraphrase is the whole game

Answers are almost never word-for-word matches. The test rewards spotting that "reductions in prescribed medication" and "fewer people taking drugs" are the same idea.
