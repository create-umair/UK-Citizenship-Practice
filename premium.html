Frequently asked questions

Is this an official government product?+
No. This is an independent study aid, not affiliated with the Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration, or gov.uk. It's designed to help you prepare, alongside — not instead of — the official handbook and requirements.
How does payment work?+
You pay once through PayPal — with your PayPal balance or, where PayPal offers it, a debit or credit card. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Access unlocks as soon as PayPal confirms the payment.
Can I use my plan on another phone or computer?+
Your access is saved in the browser you paid from. If you switch device, or clear your browser's site data, it will not carry over automatically. Keep your PayPal order ID and contact us with it and we will sort it out.
Does passing a mock exam here guarantee I'll pass the real test?+
No test can guarantee a result. Consistently scoring well above the pass mark across several mock exams is a good sign you're well prepared, but always study the official handbook as your primary source.
Is the English or IELTS practice an approved language test?+
No. It's practice material only. For your application, you'll need an approved Secure English Language Test (SELT) from a Home Office-approved provider, unless you qualify for an exemption.
